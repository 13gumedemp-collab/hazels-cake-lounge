The menu

Something for every occasion.

From show-stopping cakes to everyday home bakes, I make sweet treats for celebrations, gifting, tea time and everything in between. Choose a category below, then tell me what you have in mind.

01

Celebration cakes

Birthday, anniversary, baby shower and milestone cakes, made around your colours, theme and favourite flavours.

02

Wedding and occasion cakes

Elegant cakes for weddings, engagements, graduations and special gatherings, from intimate to statement-making.

03

Cupcakes and mini treats

Hand-iced cupcakes and smaller sweet treats for parties, gifting, offices and shared celebration tables.

04

Tea-time bakes

Muffins, scones, loaf cakes and tray bakes for relaxed gatherings, meetings and a good cup of tea.

05

Biscuits and tarts

Assorted, butter, ginger and choc chip biscuits, plus jam tarts for sharing, gifting or stocking the pantry.

06

Rusks and pantry bakes

Classic, buttermilk, all bran and raisin rusks for slow mornings, thoughtful gifts and everyday cupboards.

Have a specific flavour, celebration or idea in mind? Tell me about it and I will create something made for you.
